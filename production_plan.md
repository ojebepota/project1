# MacBook Pro M4 Max Ultimate Setup — Cinematic Production Blueprint (2025)

## 1. Narrative Arc & Creative Direction
- **Tone**: Aspirational, calm confidence. The film positions the MacBook Pro M4 Max as the apex creative workstation, blending high fashion lifestyle with cutting-edge productivity.
- **Narrative Flow**: "From inspiration to execution" — open with serene environment, build to intense multi-screen productivity, resolve with satisfied creative surveying the setup.
- **Visual Language**: Apple launch aesthetics — negative space, precise movement, premium materials, micro-detail macros.
- **Color Science**: Shoot and grade in Rec.2020 HDR10+ pipeline. Capture in S-Log3 (Sony FX6) / C-Log3 (Canon C70) / REDCODE IPP2 for maximum latitude. Apply custom Kodak Vision3-inspired LUT with teal/blue accent lift and warm skin retention.

## 3. Lighting & Environment Plan
- **Key**: 5'x7' softbox camera-left at 45°, 5600K, intensity 70%.
- **Fill**: White foam bounce camera-right, 2 stops under key, 5200K for gentle warmth.
- **Rim**: Two Astera Titan tubes behind monitors, 30° angle, set to electric blue (#0984e3) gradient.
- **Practical**: LED desk strips (mint #00cec9), monitor backlight bias, recessed ceiling lights at 30%.
- **Modifier Notes**: Use flagging to prevent glare on Liquid Retina XDR; polarizer on lens for reflections.
- **Room Styling**: Benjamin Moore Cloud White walls, European oak flooring, walnut desk with Herman Miller Sayl chair, single Bird of Paradise plant, abstract art piece centered opposite monitors.

## 4. Screen Content & Playback Workflow
- **Capture Prep**: Build live projects on MacBook to avoid post fakery. Load Final Cut Pro (8K RED timeline), Logic Pro (mixing session), Xcode (SwiftUI project), Adobe Bridge, Slack/Notion dashboards.
- **Delivery to Displays**: Use Thunderbolt 5 dock to daisy-chain four Samsung Odyssey OLED G9 monitors via Ergotron HX arms; calibrate with i1Display Pro to 6500K, 120 nits reference.
- **Screen Replacement**: Record UI plates via HDMI capture cards at native 5120x1440 per monitor + 3456x2234 for MacBook. Track screens in post using Mocha Pro. Apply bloom/glow passes matching practical lighting.
- **Performance Metrics Overlay**: Create After Effects HUD elements (CPU/GPU utilization, 120Hz ProMotion callout) with SF Pro typography.

## 5. Talent Wardrobe & Styling
- **Wardrobe Lookbook**: Lemaire charcoal merino sweater, tailored slate trousers, minimalist sneakers. Backup options in sage green and cream.

## 7. Production Schedule
### Pre-Production (Week -2 to -1)
1. Scout location, measure power circuits, and test blackout capability.
2. Build 3D previz in CineDesigner/Unreal for blocking approval.
3. Finalize shot list & storyboards (this doc) with director, DP, client.
4. Program screen content, rehearse trackpad/keyboard choreography.
5. Confirm gear rentals, talent contracts, catering, COVID compliance.

### Production Day (12-hour)
1. **Call Time 07:00** — Crew arrival, unload, safety brief.
2. **07:00-11:00** — Lighting grid, monitor rigging, calibration, camera prep.
3. **11:00-12:30** — Blocking rehearsal, wardrobe finalization, lens tests.
4. **12:30-17:00** — Principal photography following shot list, capture multiple takes at 24p & 60p.
5. **17:00-19:00** — B-roll, detail macros, social-first vertical framing.
6. **19:00-20:00** — Data wrangle (3-2-1 backup), equipment strike.

### Post-Production (Week +1 to +2)
1. Ingest & sync multi-angle footage, apply camera LUTs.
2. Offline edit in Final Cut Pro/DaVinci Resolve at 8K master timeline.
3. Integrate motion graphics & tracked screen comps (After Effects).
4. Sound design pass: layer ambient room tone, foley, UI clicks, tech ambience.
5. Compose/License ambient electronic score, mix to -14 LUFS (streaming) and -24 LKFS (broadcast).
6. Color grade in DaVinci Resolve with HDR10+ metadata, deliver Rec.2020 masters.
7. Export deliverables per platform (see Section 10) and prep thumbnail stills.

## 8. Audio & Music Direction
- **Ambient Bed**: Record subtle HVAC and city rumble via stereo pair; layer with synthesized airy textures.
- **Foley**: Capture mechanical keyboard clicks, MagSafe connection, trackpad swipes, AirPods Max placement.
- **Music**: Commission 90 BPM ambient track blending Ólafur Arnalds-style piano with evolving synth pads. Build crescendo timed to 34-52s montage.
- **Mix**: 5.1 surround master + stereo fold-down. Ensure dialogue-free track meets specs for global localization.

## 9. Post-Production Technical Specs
- **Project Resolution**: 8192x4320 (master), downscale to 7680x4320 & 3840x2160 deliverables.
- **Frame Rates**: Base 23.976p timeline; integrate 59.94p slow-motion retimed to 40% speed.
- **Codec**: ProRes 4444 XQ mezzanine, ProRes 422 HQ for client review, HEVC Main10 HDR for streaming.
- **Audio**: 24-bit/48kHz WAV stems (music, foley, ambience), Dolby Atmos ADM optional.
- **Archival**: LTO-8 backup + cloud redundancy (Frame.io/Vimeo review copies).

## 11. Risk Mitigation & QC
- Redundant screen content playback laptops & capture cards.
- UPS backup for workstation and lighting to avoid flicker during power dips.
- Schedule buffer for recalibrating HDR monitors and avoiding burn-in.
- Implement QC checklist: dead pixel scan, screen flicker verification, HDR metadata validation, audio loudness compliance.

## 12. Approval & Review Workflow
1. **Rough Cut (Day +4)** — internal review via Frame.io, gather timecoded notes.
2. **Fine Cut (Day +7)** — incorporate motion graphics, send to client.
3. **Picture Lock (Day +9)** — freeze edit, proceed with final color & mix.
4. **Master Delivery (Day +12)** — provide download links + backup drives. Include LUTs, project files, documentation.
